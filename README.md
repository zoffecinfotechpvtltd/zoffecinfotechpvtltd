## About Us

**Zoffec Infotech Pvt. Ltd.** is a Mumbai-based managed IT & cybersecurity provider, serving as a long-term digital security partner for Indian organizations — delivering enterprise-grade cybersecurity with practical governance across infrastructure, compliance, cloud, endpoint, and AI.

Our group also builds **Zoffec Aegis** (via **ZTPL — Zoffec Technologies Private Limited**), a multi-tenant GRC platform purpose-built for SEBI-regulated entities and their service providers.

> **Vision** — To be a trusted cybersecurity partner for Indian organizations that need reliable protection, governance, and long-term digital resilience.
>
> **Mission** — Simplify cybersecurity and compliance through expert assessment, disciplined implementation, managed support, and clear business-aligned reporting.
